# Style Recipes

A recipe describes **style only, never the subject**. Reuse with:
`Use this style: [RECIPE]. New subject: [...]. Text: "[...]".`
Keep 3–4 per brand. Save winners from your own work here (see `docs/07-workflow.md`).

---

### HOT SALE
Saturated orange gradient #FF7A00→#E63E00, white extra-bold neo-grotesk headline stacked
3 lines top-left, one tilted yellow #FFD400 offer badge with black type, white pill CTA.
Product large and cropped by the right edge, soft studio light. 7% margins, 30% calm space.
Never: sparkles, confetti, extra products, purple.

### FAST FOOD BOLD
Solid brand red #C8102E, one oversized yellow brand curve bleeding off a corner, two-tone
rounded extra-bold headline (white + #FFC72C). Food hero large, front three-quarter, warm
45° side light, real texture, cropped by bottom edge. Never: steam, smoke, extra items.

### NIGHT SPORT
Charcoal #121212, one diagonal orange #FF5A1F light streak, heavy condensed italic all-caps
white headline with 0.9 leading, product at low dynamic angle on wet asphalt with coloured
rim light. Never: neon glow on text, particles.

### PREMIUM TECH
Off-white #F5F5F7, near-black neo-grotesk, very generous whitespace (45%+), tight text
groups, black + outlined pill buttons, product cropped by edges with soft top light and
gentle edge reflections. Never: gradients, glow, floating UI, lens flare.

### COLD & DYNAMIC
Deep blue #0B2A6F radial gradient, oversized object tilted ~20° and cropped off-frame,
condensation droplets, hard backlight rim, heavy italic caps headline on the left third.
Never: splash, ice cubes, sparkles.

### LIFESTYLE SKY
Full-bleed natural photo, low angle 85mm, subject looking off-frame toward the text, clear
soft sky as calm text area, golden-hour side light, natural skin texture; headline mixes
upright bold + italic of one sans. Never: plastic skin, stock smile at camera.

### WORSHIP NIGHT
Deep navy #0E1A3A, single soft warm-gold light beam from top centre, subtle film grain,
huge cream high-contrast serif headline, gold all-caps kicker with wide tracking, small
light-sans details bottom-left. Never: doves, crosses, clip-art, rays, multiple fonts.

### EDITORIAL CAROUSEL
Warm off-white #FAF8F5, one neo-grotesk family, two-line titles (black line + grey line),
grey intro + black instruction sentence, before/after cards with rounded corners, black and
grey pill labels, 8% margins. Never: gradients, decoration, shadows beyond a soft card shadow.

### FRESH & NATURAL
Cream #F6F1E7, leaf green #2F7D32 accent, dark green #14361A text, humanist serif headline,
top-down natural-light flat-lay, real textures, lots of air. Never: glossy look, oversaturation.

### LUXURY NOIR
Black #0A0A0A, gold #C9A24B thin details, ivory didone serif headline with wide tracking,
single product under a hard top spotlight with deep shadows, 50% empty space.
Never: glitter, bokeh, gold sparkles, chrome text.
