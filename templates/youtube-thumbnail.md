# Template: YouTube Thumbnail (inspiration + your real photo + video info)

Attach the images **in this order**:
1. the **inspiration thumbnail** you want yours to look like
2. **your real photo** (full-resolution camera/phone photo — not a screenshot)
3. *(optional)* real **screenshots / product photo** of what the video shows
4. *(optional)* your channel **logo** and/or 2–3 of your **recent thumbnails**

Then paste this and fill in the brackets:

```text
Read and follow: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MUKE-coder/pro-design-prompts/main/AI.md

Making: YouTube thumbnail
Posting on: YouTube (long-form video)   [+ any other platform]

Image 1 = inspiration thumbnail. Image 2 = my real photo. [Image 3 = screenshot of …] [Image 4 = my logo]

Video title: [...]
Video description:
[...]
Timestamps:
[00:00 ...]
[...]

Channel colours / font: [#hex, #hex / "use the inspiration's"]
Must not include: [...]
```

**What happens next:**
1. The AI reads your video and shows a **Video Read** (promise, payoff moment, curiosity gap, proof, emotion).
2. It asks you **at least 6 questions**, each with a suggested default, plus an **Assets I Need** list
   (better photo, screenshots, logo, recent thumbnails…). Reply `ok`, or e.g. `1 ok, 2 use B, 4 sending new photo`.
3. It breaks down the inspiration, shows **Thumbnail Decisions**, then gives you the prompt with an
   **UPLOAD ORDER** block at the top (which image is IMAGE 1, 2, 3…).
4. Paste that prompt into ChatGPT (or Gemini) with the images in that order.

## Follow-ups that work

| You want | Say |
|---|---|
| Face changed / smoothed | "My face was altered. Use image 2 exactly — same face, same skin texture, no retouching. Change nothing else." |
| Looks too AI | "Do a human-made audit: name the 3 things that most look AI-generated, remove them, keep everything else identical." |
| Light looks pasted | "Relight the background to match the light already on my face (key from the [left], [warm]). Remove the coloured rim light." |
| Text too small | "Make the text 30% larger and cut it to [2] words: "…". Keep everything else identical." |
| Dashboard as a mockup | "Show IMAGE n [in a browser window / on a laptop / as a zoomed callout] — exactly as provided, no retyped UI." |
| A/B test | "Same thumbnail, variation B: only change the text to "…"." |
| Shorts version | "Re-lay this out as a 9:16 Shorts cover. Keep text in the centre 60%." |

## When ChatGPT keeps drifting your face
Ask for the scene **without you** ("leave the right 45% as an empty background matched to this
lighting"), then place your real cut-out photo and the text yourself in Photopea / Photoshop / Canva.
That's the most human-looking result you can get.
