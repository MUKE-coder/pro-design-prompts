# Template: Local Business Promo

Restaurants, salons, shops, clinics, schools, services, real estate.
Goal: **one offer, one picture, one action** — and it must look like *this* business, not any
business in the category.

## Answer these 5 first
1. What's the ONE offer or message? ("Free delivery this week", "Braids from UGX 40,000")
2. What ONE picture proves it? (one dish, one hairstyle, one room, one smiling client)
3. What should people DO? (call, WhatsApp, visit, book)
4. What makes you different from the shop next door? (put that in the headline or visual)
5. What should never appear? (other brands, alcohol, crowded scenes…)

## Design Decisions
```text
FORMAT:     [4:5 feed / 9:16 WhatsApp status / A5 handout]
HERO:       [one signature dish / one finished hairstyle / the storefront] — 55% of canvas
HIERARCHY:  1 [visual] → 2 [offer headline] → 3 [one detail line] → 4 [WhatsApp/call CTA]
LAYOUT:     [Headline-top / hero-bottom] or [Split]
TYPE:       ONE family: [rounded bold sans for food / refined serif for beauty / geometric sans for services]
COLOUR:     brand [#hex] background, [#hex] accent, [#hex] text
LIGHT:      [warm 45° side light, real texture]
BREAK:      [extreme close-up crop / top-down flat-lay / subject cropped by edge]
SPACE:      35% calm; space reserved top-left for logo
NO:         [ban list + category clichés]
```

## Prompt
```text
Create a flat 2D promotional social media design for a [BUSINESS TYPE] called "[NAME]",
edge-to-edge, [4:5 portrait]. Not a mockup.

BACKGROUND: solid [#hex brand colour], plain.

HERO: [ONE subject, described precisely — e.g. "a single plate of grilled tilapia with
kachumbari and a lemon wedge on a matte black plate"], shot [top-down / 45° / close-up macro],
[warm side light from the left], real textures, true-to-life colour. Positioned [lower half,
slightly right], about 55% of the canvas, [cropped by the bottom edge].

TEXT — render exactly as written, no other text anywhere:
- Top-left, very large, [rounded extra-bold sans], [white], 2 lines: "[OFFER HEADLINE]"
- Below, small regular: "[ONE DETAIL LINE]"
- Bottom-left, a [white pill] with [brand-colour] text: "[WhatsApp 0700 000 000]"
- Leave an empty space top-right, 12% of width, for the logo.

LAYOUT: 7% margins, left-aligned text, headline and detail grouped, CTA separated,
35% empty background.

STYLE: [modern independent restaurant brand / premium salon editorial / friendly
neighbourhood service], warm, honest, specific — not stock photography.

DO NOT INCLUDE: steam, smoke, extra dishes, cutlery clutter, chalkboards, people,
stock-photo smiles, sparkles, glow, fake logos, extra text, drop shadows on text.
```

## Trust tip
For real food, real staff and real premises, a **real photo** builds more trust. Upload your
photo and say: *"Use the uploaded photo exactly as is. Design the layout and text around it.
Do not redraw, retouch or change the food/person."*
