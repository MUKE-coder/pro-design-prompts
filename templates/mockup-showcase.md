# Template: Website / App Mockup Showcase

Attach **in this order**:
1. *(optional)* your **mockup template** (Freepik, Pinterest, a PSD preview, any image you like)
2. *(optional)* your **logo**
3. your **real screenshots**, one per device (desktop → tablet → phone), captured as in the
   [capture guide](../docs/12-mockups.md#capture-guide-do-this-well-and-everything-else-gets-easy)
4. *(optional)* a **real photo of you** if you want a person in the scene

Then paste:

```text
Read and follow: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MUKE-coder/pro-design-prompts/main/AI.md

Making: website/app mockup showcase
Posting on: [Behance / Dribbble / Instagram / LinkedIn / case study page / client handover]
Product: [what it is, who it's for, the one thing the mockup should prove]
Template: [attached as image 1 / none — suggest recipes from the mockup library]
Screens attached: [image 2 = desktop home, image 3 = phone home, …]
Person: [none / my real photo / a generated model]
Brand: [colours, logo attached, any caption or headline on the mockup]
```

## What happens
1. **Product Read** — what it is, who sees it, the one thing to prove, which screens are missing.
2. **Template** — your template broken down (devices, angles, light, AI tells to remove), or 2–3
   recipes from the library (M01–M15) to pick from.
3. **Questions + SCREENS I NEED** — exact capture sizes per device. Reply `ok` or change things.
4. **WOW concept + Photoshop layer plan**, and the **route**:
   - *one-step* — the AI renders the screens too (1–2 big, simple screens);
   - *two-step* — the AI renders the scene with blank screens and you place your real screenshots
     in Photopea with the placement recipe (best for 3+ devices or text-heavy UIs).
5. The prompt, starting with the **UPLOAD ORDER**, ending with a **CONTENT LOCK**.

## Follow-ups
| Problem | Say |
|---|---|
| UI text garbled | "Switch to two-step: same scene, all screens blank mid-grey #8A8F98 with a slight gloss. Then give me the placement recipe." |
| Screens too dark / washed out | "Keep everything; make the screens the brightest thing in the frame (white ≈ 93%), environment one stop darker, reflections off the text." |
| Device has a brand logo | "Remove all brand logos from the devices; keep everything else identical." |
| Too plain | "WOW PASS: add [a brand-colour arc behind the devices / a hand holding the phone / giant 01 numeral], keep the screens exact." |
