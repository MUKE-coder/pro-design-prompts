# Reference Mode: Turning a Design You Like Into Your Own

Most real design work starts with "make it like this, but for us". AI tools ruin this in two
ways: they **copy too much** (the other company's text, phone number, even their logo) or **too
little** (they keep the vibe but lose the layout, the headline treatment and the details that made
it good). Reference Mode fixes both by separating **design** from **content**.

| Copy from the reference (the DESIGN) | Never copy (the CONTENT) |
|---|---|
| Aspect ratio, layout zones, proportions | Their logo or brand name |
| Hierarchy and reading order | Their headline, slogans, any text |
| Headline treatment (size, 3D, overlap) | Phone numbers, handles, websites |
| Type style per level | The real person's identity |
| Colour *roles* and light/dark balance | Trademarked characters or marks |
| Lighting, finish (glass, 3D, flat) | |
| Element *types*, *count* and positions | |

## Worked example — the "NEW WEEK" creative-agency poster

### Template Breakdown
```
FORMAT:      4:5 portrait
BACKGROUND:  off-white #F4F5F7 with very faint grid lines and tiny "+" marks (top 65%);
             bottom ~35% is a royal-blue (#1E3BD6 → #2F5BFF) curved dome spanning full width,
             top edge arcs upward towards the centre
LAYOUT:      logo top-left · kicker + giant headline top-centre (~10–55% of height) ·
             hero centre sitting on the dome edge · service pills scattered on the dome ·
             footer pills bottom-left and bottom-right
HIERARCHY:   1 "NEW WEEK" headline → 2 hero woman → 3 kicker → 4 service pills → 5 footer contacts
HEADLINE:    two lines, ultra-bold condensed caps, ~45% of canvas height combined, 3D folded/
             extruded paper look; "NEW" deep blue with lighter-blue fold faces; "WEEK" white with
             blue extrusion; centred
TYPE:        kicker small bold geometric sans, navy, "BRAND" heavier; pills medium sans in
             white/navy; footer small caps sans
HERO:        young woman with an afro-puff bun and glasses, blue-and-white varsity jacket, white top,
             wide-leg jeans, blue-white sneakers; sits on the dome's top edge working on a laptop;
             in FRONT of the lower part of "WEEK"; legs dangle forward so sneakers come toward camera
SUPPORT:     2 frosted-glass pills on the light area ("Fully Active", "Let's Design");
             5 glass pills on the blue dome (services); ~6 floating 3D icons (drawing tablet + stylus,
             colour-swatch tile, pixel cursor, bell, lightning-head, hand-with-puzzle); 2 thin dotted arcs
LOGO SLOT:   top-left, ~14% of width, circular blue badge
FOOTER:      outlined pill with company name (bottom-left), outlined pill with phone (bottom-right)
COLOUR ROLES: background off-white · primary royal blue · secondary navy · text white/navy
LIGHT/DEPTH: soft studio light from the top, soft contact shadows, blue reflections, glassmorphism
             pills with blur, shallow depth on floating icons
THE BREAK:   hero sits in front of the giant headline and her sneakers break the picture plane
```

### Content Map (example for a fictional client)
| Slot | Reference (don't copy) | New content |
|---|---|---|
| Logo top-left | their emblem | **uploaded logo, exact** |
| Kicker | "IT'S A BRAND" | "IT'S A FRESH" |
| Headline | "NEW" / "WEEK" | "NEW" / "MONTH" *(5 letters → headline scaled down ~10%)* |
| Light-area pills (2) | "Fully Active", "Let's Design" | "Open for Projects", "Let's Build" |
| Dome pills (5) | Branding, Posters, Motions, illustrations, Campaigns | Websites, Apps, Branding, UI/UX, SEO |
| Footer left | company name | "[YOUR COMPANY]" |
| Footer right | phone | "[+256 7XX XXX XXX]" |

### Brand Adaptation
- Colours: client brand is green #0F7B4A + lime #C6F432 → dome becomes green, "NEW" green,
  extrusions lime-tinted, varsity jacket green-and-white. Same light/dark balance.
- Hero: new person, same pose/scale/placement, outfit recoloured to brand.
- Icons: swapped to the client's world — code-brackets tile, phone mock-up, cursor, browser window,
  bell, lightning (no rocket cliché). Same count (6).
- Improved vs reference: lowercase "illustrations" pill fixed to title case for consistency.

## Tips for choosing good references
- Save references in a **swipe folder** with one line on *why* you like each.
- A reference with **lots of text** will produce text errors — prefer ones with ≤ 25 words.
- Match the **slot lengths**: a reference with a 3-letter headline word needs a short word from you.
- One reference per design. Mixing two references = muddled result. If you must, say exactly what to
  take from each ("layout from image 1, colours from image 3").
- Upload the **highest quality** version of the reference you can find.
