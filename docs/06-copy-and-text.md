# Copy: Write the Words Before the Layout

The layout follows the copy. If the copy is a product description, the design will be a
product description. Decide the **voice** first, write the **headline** in that voice, then design.

---

## 1. Pick one voice

| Voice | Headline example |
|---|---|
| Bold & loud | "70% OFF. THIS WEEKEND ONLY." |
| Premium & calm | "Built for what's next." |
| Playful | "Lunch, but make it crunchy." |
| Warm & human | "Made the way Mum made it." |
| Clinical & data-led | "2× faster. Half the cost." |
| Spiritual & uplifting | "A Night of Worship." |
| Street & energetic | "RUN FURTHER." |

---

## 2. The text budget (single image)

| Block | Max | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Kicker (optional) | 3 words | "SUNDAY 14 DEC" |
| Headline | 6 words | "FUEL YOUR DAY" |
| Subline | 12 words | "Boosts energy and keeps you going." |
| Offer | 4 words | "Up to 70% Off" |
| CTA | 3 words | "Shop Now →" |
| Details | 1–3 short lines | "Kampala Serena · 6PM · UGX 50,000" |

**Total ≤ ~25 words.** Everything else goes in the caption.

---

## 3. Headline formulas

- **Benefit verb**: "Run Further" · "Fuel Your Day" · "Sleep Deeper"
- **Category → feeling**: "Jeans" → "Timeless Style"
- **Number-led**: "70% Off" · "3 Days. 40 Artists." · "From UGX 15,000"
- **Event name as poster**: "WORSHIP NIGHT 2026"
- **Short promise**: "Built for what's next."

Bad: "Amazon Great Indian Festival Sale" (category + brand + event, no hierarchy).
Good: "Great Indian Festival" + badge "Up to 70% Off".

---

## 4. Avoid hype & invented facts

- Ban: *elevate, unlock, unleash, revolutionise, seamless, cutting-edge, next-level, game-changer*.
- Never let the AI invent prices, dates, phone numbers, discounts, addresses, awards or claims.
  If a fact is missing → placeholder `[PHONE]`, then fill in later.
- After generation, verify every **letter, number, price, date, time, name** — twice.

---

## 5. Getting 3 headline options

Ask your chat model:
```text
Write 5 headline options for this design in a [VOICE] voice. Each ≤ 5 words. No hype
words. One must be number-led, one must be a benefit verb, one must be a feeling.
```
Pick one. Then design around it.
