# YouTube Thumbnails

A thumbnail is not a small flyer. It's seen for half a second, at the size of a postage stamp,
next to your title, surrounded by competitors. Its one job: **make the click feel inevitable —
honestly.**

The AI runs this as **Thumbnail Mode** ([AI.md §5](../AI.md)). The copy-paste message is in
[templates/youtube-thumbnail.md](../templates/youtube-thumbnail.md).

---

## The workflow

```
1. INSPIRATION   find a thumbnail you like (YouTube, a competitor, a swipe folder)
2. YOUR PHOTO    a real camera/phone photo of you — 2–3 expressions is ideal
3. VIDEO INFO    title, description, timestamps
4. SEND          AI.md link + images + info  →  AI asks ≥ 6 questions + asks for assets
5. ANSWER        reply "ok" to the defaults or change what you want; send extra assets
6. GET           Video Read → Breakdown → Decisions → the prompt (starts with the UPLOAD ORDER)
7. GENERATE      paste the prompt into ChatGPT with the images in that order
8. REVIEW        deletion pass + squint test → one fix at a time
```

## Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | **1280×720**, 16:9 (min 640 px wide), JPG/PNG, under 2 MB |
| Shown at | as small as ~168×94 (sidebar / mobile) — design for that |
| Covered | **bottom-right corner** (duration stamp) — no text or face there |
| Shorts cover | 9:16, keep text in the centre 60% |

## The rules

1. **Three elements max** — your face + a few words + one proof object.
2. **≤ 4 words** — huge, one bold font, flat colour. Complements the title, never repeats it.
   No text is often better.
3. **Face 30–40% of the height**, eyes sharp, one honest expression matching the video's emotion.
4. **Real proof beats decoration** — a real screenshot, result, product or object from the video
   instead of icon lists, glowing panels and arrows.
5. **Squint test** — shrink it to 168×94. Can you read it and see your face? If not, cut.
6. **Stand out** — look at the other thumbnails for your topic. If they're all dark blue, don't be.
7. **Honest** — everything shown is in the video. Curiosity yes, lies no.
8. **Human-made** — your real face untouched, light matched to your photo, no glass/sparkles, any glow or rim light motivated
   and matched ([docs/11](11-photoshop-master.md))
   ([docs/09](09-human-made-look.md)).

## Title + thumbnail work as a pair

| Title says | Thumbnail shows |
|---|---|
| "I Built a Full-Stack App in 10 Minutes" | the finished app screenshot + "10 MIN?" |
| "The Go Framework Nobody Talks About" | your face + the terminal command + "WAIT…" |
| "Stop Using X" | X crossed out (real logo only if you're allowed) + your face |

The thumbnail **adds** something the title doesn't: the proof, the emotion, or the tease.

## Shooting your photo (2 minutes, phone is fine)

- Face a window (soft light from one side or front). No overhead room light mixing in.
- Phone at eye level or slightly above, 1–1.5 m away, portrait mode **off**, shoulders up.
- Plain-ish background. Wear a colour that contrasts with your channel colours.
- Shoot 10+ frames across 2–3 expressions (curious, surprised, confident). Pick the sharpest eyes.
- Send the original file, not a screenshot.
