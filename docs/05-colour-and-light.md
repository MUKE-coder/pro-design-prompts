# Colour & Light

## 1. Colour: max 3 + neutrals, with roles

Never say "colourful" or "vibrant colours". Give each colour a **job** and a **hex**.

```text
Palette: background deep red #C8102E (60%), accent golden yellow #FFC72C (10%),
text white #FFFFFF (30% of text). No other colours except the natural colours of the food.
```

**60-30-10 rule**: 60% dominant (background), 30% secondary (big shapes/photo), 10% accent (CTA, offer, one word).

### Palette starters

| Mood | Background | Accent | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hot sale / energy | Orange #FF6A00 → #E63E00 | Yellow #FFD400 | White |
| Appetite / fast food | Red #C8102E | Yellow #FFC72C | White |
| Premium tech | Off-white #F5F5F7 | One product colour | Near-black #1D1D1F |
| Sport / night | Charcoal #121212 | Electric orange #FF5A1F | White |
| Energy drink / cold | Deep blue #0B2A6F | Silver / red | White |
| Fresh / health | Cream #F6F1E7 | Leaf green #2F7D32 | Dark green #14361A |
| Luxury | Black #0A0A0A | Gold #C9A24B | Ivory #F3EBDD |
| Church / gospel event | Deep navy #0E1A3A or warm black | Warm gold #E2B04A | Cream |
| Kids / playful | Sky blue #4FC3F7 | Sunny yellow #FFD54F + coral | Navy |
| Fintech / trust | White | Royal blue #1F4BFF | Ink #0B1020 |

### Colour rules
- **Text contrast**: light text on dark or dark on light — aim for WCAG 4.5:1.
- **Brand colour as background**, not as a timid accent, when you want boldness.
- **Gradients**: only between two *neighbouring* hues of the same colour (orange → deep orange,
  blue → navy). Never purple → cyan "AI gradient".
- **Neutrals aren't grey-grey**: warm off-white (#FAF8F5) or cool off-white (#F5F7FA) look intentional.

## 2. Light: describe it like a photographer

"Good lighting" means nothing to a model. Name the setup.

| Look | Prompt phrase |
|---|---|
| Clean product studio | "softbox key light from upper left, white bounce fill on right, subtle shadow under product" |
| Dramatic product | "hard single key light from the side, deep shadows, thin rim light outlining the edges" |
| Premium tech | "soft diffused top light, gentle gradient reflections along the edges, no harsh highlights" |
| Cold drink | "backlit, condensation droplets catching light, cool blue rim light" |
| Food appetite | "warm side light at 45°, soft shadows, visible texture, slight sheen on sauce" |
| Lifestyle outdoor | "late afternoon golden-hour sunlight from the left, natural shadows" |
| Night / sport | "coloured rim light in #FF5A1F from behind, dark environment, wet reflective ground" |
| Portrait | "Rembrandt lighting, soft key at 45°, natural skin texture" |

### Separation
Always separate the hero from the background: **rim light**, **contrast** (dark product on light bg),
or **shadow** under the product. Otherwise it looks pasted on.

## 3. Realism cues (anti-plastic)

Add these for photographic elements:
- "natural skin texture with visible pores, subtle imperfections"
- "realistic materials: brushed aluminium, cotton weave, sesame seeds with real texture"
- "shot on a full-frame camera, 85mm lens, f/2.8" (gives believable depth of field)
- "true-to-life colour, not oversaturated"
- "a single, physically plausible light source direction"
